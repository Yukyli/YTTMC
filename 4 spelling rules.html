🔤 The Four Rules of Spelling 🔤

Learn to spell tricky words in a fun way!

👋 Hello! What will we learn?

Today we will learn 4 special rules to help us spell many English words.

These rules work for about 80% of English words! That's 8 out of every 10 words. 🎯

Are you ready? Let's go! 🚀

📚 First, some special words:

Vowels = a e i o u
All other letters are called consonants.
Syllables = the "beats" in a word. Clap as you say a word! 👏
Example: com-pu-ter has 3 beats = 3 syllables.
Rule 1

Every Syllable Has a Vowel

All words are made of syllables (beats).

Every syllable must have a vowel (a, e, i, o, u).

Let's clap the beats! 👏
cat (1 beat) rab-bit (2 beats) com-pu-ter (3 beats)

👆 Click the words to hear them!

Rule 2

Vowels Say 2 Sounds

Each single vowel can say two sounds:

1. Their NAME (long sound) 📛
ace, emu, ice, open, use
2. Their SOUND (short sound) 🔊
at, egg, in, on, ugly
Say "A" — that's its name.
Say "æ" (like in cat) — that's its sound.
Rule 3

Closed Syllables → Vowel Says its SOUND

A closed syllable has a consonant AFTER the vowel. The door is closed! 🚪

The vowel says its short sound.

cat ink sock um-brel-la

👆 See how a consonant comes after the vowel? That "closes" it in!

Rule 4

Open Syllables → Vowel Says its NAME

An open syllable ends with the vowel. The door is open! 🚪✨

The vowel says its long name.

o/pen     ra/zor

In o/pen: "o" is open, so it says its name "Oh".
In ra/zor: "ra" is open, so it says its name "Ray".

🎮 Let's Play! Quiz Time!

Q1: How many syllables are in the word "computer"?

Q2: In "cat", is the "a" open or closed?

Q3: In "o/pen", is the "o" open or closed?

✍️ How to Spell Tricky Words

When you try to spell a word, ask yourself:

👀 Does it LOOK right?
🔤 Are the letters together in a way I have seen before?

Example: Spelling "computer" 💻

Click to see the helpful clues!
  • ✅ It is a 3-syllable word
  • ✅ It has one open syllable (pu → says "pyoo")
  • ✅ The "ter" ending rhymes with "her"
  • ✅ It has 8 letters
So: com + pu + ter = computer 🎉

🌟 Great Tips from Your Teacher

Say: "You have a go — I'll help you!" 🤝
Don't say: "Look it up in a dictionary." 📕❌
Try rhyming words!
If you know light, you can spell right and tight! ✨
Break big words into syllables.
hos + pi + tal = hospital 🏥
frag + ment = fragment 🧩

  • ✅ Vowels say 2 sounds: a name and a sound
  • ✅ Closed syllables → vowel says its sound
  • ✅ Open syllables → vowel says its name
