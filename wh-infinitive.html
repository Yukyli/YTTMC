Which Wh-words Can We Use?

✅
YES — Use these:
what • who • which
when • where • how
❌
NO — Never with:
why
❌ I don't know why to go.
✅ I don't know why I should go.

  • 2. Tell me how I can solve this.

⚠️ Mistake #1: Word Order

❌ Wrong:
I don't know to do what.
I don't know what I to do.
✅ Correct:
I don't know what to do.
🧠 Wh-word FIRST, then to + verb
11 / 13

⚠️ Mistake #2: Negative Form

Wh-word + not + to + verb
❌ Wrong:
The mother told the child where to not go.
✅ Correct:
The mother told the child where not to go.
He learned what not to say in an interview.
I know who not to trust.
12 / 13

⚠️ Mistake #3: Using "why"

❌ Wrong:
I don't know why to leave.
✅ Correct:
I don't know why I should leave.
🚫 NO "why" with to-infinitive!
