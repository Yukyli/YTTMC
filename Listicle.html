3 Types of Questions

Your questionnaire must use a mix of three question types:

✅❌
① Yes / No
Quick answers
📅
② Frequency
How often?
❓
③ Wh- Questions (open answers)
What / When / Where / Who / Why / How

① Yes / No Questions

Structure: Do / Does + subject + verb + ... ?
Structure: Have / Has + subject + past participle + ... ?

✅ Examples:

Do you have spots on your face?
Does your sister use a hair conditioner?
Have you ever had dandruff?

⚠️ Common Mistake:

❌ You like apples? (missing "Do")
✅ Do you like apples?

② Frequency Questions

Structure: How often + do / does + subject + verb + ... ?

Possible Answers:

always
usually
often
sometimes
rarely
never
twice a day
once a week

✅ Examples:

How often do you wash your hair?
How often does your brother drink water?

③ Wh- Questions

Six question words you should remember:

What
When
Where
Who
Why
How
Structure: Wh-word + do / does + subject + verb + ... ?
With "be" verb: Wh-word + is / are + subject + ... ?

✅ Examples:

What do you usually drink in the morning?
When is the best time to brush your teeth?

(Click to reveal)

(Click to reveal)

🤢 What is a SICK Question?

A SICK question is a BAD question that we should avoid!

😡
RUDE
Makes the person feel embarrassed or attacked.
🙄
OBVIOUS
The answer is so obvious — it gives no useful information.
🎯
LEADING
Pushes the person to give a certain answer.
🔒
TOO PERSONAL / TWO-IN-ONE
Asks something private, OR asks two things at once.

📝 What is a Listicle? Day 2

A listicle = an article written as a list. Very popular in newspapers and on the internet!

📰 Real Examples:

• "5 Ways to Get Better Sleep"

• "7 Habits of Successful Students"

• "10 Foods That Make Your Skin Glow"

✨ Your Task:

Write 5 tips for the school newspaper based on your survey results.

Each tip = 1 advice sentence + 1 short reason.

📌 Sample Tip:

Tip: Wash your hair at least 3 times a week.
Why: Our survey shows 4 out of 10 classmates have oily hair because they wash their hair only once a week.
