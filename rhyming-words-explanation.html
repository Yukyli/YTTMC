🤔 Section 1: What is a Rhyme?
👂

Rhyming words are words that have the SAME ending sound. 🔊

They don't have to be spelled the same — what matters is how they SOUND when we say them out loud!

⚠️ Section 4: Watch Out!

🚨 Important! Some words look similar but DON'T rhyme.

Some words look different but DO rhyme. Use your EARS, not just your eyes! 👂

💡 Section 6: Top Tips for Rhyming!
1️⃣
Say it OUT LOUD. 🗣️ Whisper the words to yourself and listen to the ending.
2️⃣
Listen to the END. 👂 Ignore the beginning sounds — focus on the last sound only!
3️⃣
Use your EARS, not eyes. 👀➡️👂 Spelling can trick you. Rhymes are about SOUND.
4️⃣
Try changing the first letter. 🔤 Cat → bat → hat → mat. They all rhyme!
