🗣️ Group Discussion Rules

✅ Do

  • 👄 Speak up
  • 👁️ Maintain eye contact
  • 🔄 Keep the conversation going
  • 👂 Listen and respond
  • 💡 Give your ideas with reasons
  • 😊 Smile

❌ Don't

  • 😶 Be passive
  • 😤 Be rude
  • 📄 Stare at the paper
🆘 Rescue Cards

What are they?

Each group gets rescue slips. If you are stuck and don't know what to say, you can use a rescue slip to help you.

When to use them?

When you can't think of what to say, or when the conversation stops.

Remember:

Using a rescue slip is not losing — it's a smart move to keep the discussion going! 💪

🆘 Rescue Phrases — Use these when you're stuck!
👂 Problem: I can't hear clearly
"Sorry, could you say that again?"
🔀 Problem: Someone is off topic
"That's interesting, but maybe we should go back to the question?"
😶 Problem: No one is speaking
"[Name], what do you think?"
⏭️ Problem: We want to move to the next question
"Shall we move on to the next question?"

🎂 Planning a Birthday Party for Your Best Friend

You are talking to your classmates about planning a birthday party for your best friend.

Discussion Questions:

  • Where should we hold the party? (e.g. restaurant, home, park)
  • What food should we prepare? (e.g. pizza, cake, snacks)
  • What gift should we buy? (e.g. something useful, something fun)
  • What activities should we plan? (e.g. games, karaoke, movie)
  • Anything else you think is important.
✏️ My Ideas:
Write your key words and short notes here during preparation time...

Useful Words:

venue
the place where the party is held
"I think the best venue is a restaurant."
budget
the amount of money you can spend
"Our budget is about $500."
decoration
things to make a place look nice
"We should buy some decorations."
surprise
something unexpected and exciting
"Let's make it a surprise party!"
invitation
asking someone to come to the party
"Should we send invitations by WhatsApp?"
celebrate
to do something special for a happy event
"We want to celebrate her birthday."
homemade
made at home, not bought
"A homemade cake is more meaningful."
thoughtful
showing care about what someone likes
"A thoughtful gift is better than an expensive one."
⚡ Discussion Rules
  • Use your phrase slips during discussion
  • Each group has rescue slips if stuck
  • Agree/disagree with NO reason = 0 points
  • Elaborate with reasons = earn points!
  • We calculate group points at the end
🎯 The goal: Keep talking and give reasons!

🚌 Planning a Class Trip Together

You are talking to your classmates about planning a class trip together.

Discussion Questions:

  • Where should we go? (e.g. beach, theme park, museum, countryside)
  • How should we get there? (e.g. bus, MTR, walking)
  • What activities should we do? (e.g. games, BBQ, sightseeing)
  • What should we bring? (e.g. food, camera, sunscreen)
  • Anything else you think is important.
✏️ My Ideas:
Write your key words and short notes here during preparation time...

Useful Words:

destination
the place you are travelling to
"I think the best destination is the beach."
transport
how you get there (bus, MTR, etc.)
"We should take the bus because it's cheaper."
outdoor
done outside, not in a building
"Outdoor activities are more fun."
vote
to choose by letting everyone decide
"Let's vote for the best place."
suggest
to give an idea for others to think about
"I suggest we go to a theme park."
prefer
to like one thing more than another
"I prefer the beach to hiking."
affordable
not too expensive
"A picnic is more affordable."
memorable
something you will remember for a long time
"I want to do something memorable."

📄 Take-Home Expression Sheet

🎤 Opening the Discussion

  • Hello, everyone. Today, we are talking about ___.
  • Let's talk about ___.
  • I'd like to start by talking about ___. In my opinion, ___ because ___.
  • First, let's discuss ___. I think ___ because ___. Does anyone agree or disagree?

💬 Giving Your Opinion

  • I think ___.
  • I also think that ___.
  • How about ___?
  • Maybe we can ___.
  • I think ___ because ___.

👍 Agreeing

  • I agree with you.
  • That is a good idea.
  • I agree with you because ___.
  • That is a good idea because ___.
  • I agree with [Name] because ___. I'd also like to add that ___.
  • That's a great point because ___. We could also ___.

😣 Disagreeing (Politely)

  • I don't think so.
  • I have a different idea.
  • I see your point, but I think ___.
  • That's true, but don't forget that ___.
  • That is an interesting point. However, I believe ___ because ___.
  • You make a good point. On the other hand, ___.
  • I understand what you mean, but have you considered ___?
  • While I agree about ___, I think ___ would be better because ___.

🗣️ Inviting Others to Speak

  • Do you agree, [Name]?
  • Do you think so, [Name]?
  • What do you think, [Name]?
  • Do you have any idea, [Name]?

📋 Closing / Summarising

  • To sum up, we think ___.
  • In conclusion, ___.
  • To sum up, most of us think ___ because ___.
  • To summarise, we discussed ___ and decided that ___.
  • To sum up, some of us think ___ while others believe ___.
  • That's the end of our discussion. Thank you.
